# FRET

One window for the two FRET modelling tools.

## The tools

| Tool | Use it to |
|---|---|
| Docking & Screening | FRET-restrained rigid-body docking, refinement and structure-library screening (IMP + IMP.bff). |
| Pair Selection | Choose the most informative FRET pairs to tell the conformations of a trajectory apart. |

## Moving around

Select a tool in the list. Each tool keeps its settings and results while the other is shown, and has its own **Guide** and **Help** buttons.
