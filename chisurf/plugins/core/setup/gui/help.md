# Settings

The Settings window collects every settings destination of ChiSurf. The list on the left starts with the Getting Started assistant; each other entry opens the dedicated panel of its tool: ChiSurf Settings, Acquisition, Styles, Plots, Models, User Editor, AI Settings, Plugins, Updates, Packages, Channel Definition, FCS Definitions, TTTR LUT Tools and Plugin Check.

## Search

Type into the search box to keep only the destinations whose name contains the text. Enter opens the first match. The current destination stays open when it is filtered out of the list.

## Back, Next and fast-forward

The buttons at the bottom right walk the list in order. Back and Next are greyed at the ends. The double arrow visits every remaining destination one after another and says so in the status line; press it again, press Back or pick a destination by hand to stop.

## Hosted panels

Each destination keeps its own state while you look at another: an unsaved edit, a selected row, the open section. The mouse wheel, keys and dropped files go to the panel under the pointer or the one you clicked last. Every panel has its own Help and Guide buttons; the ? and Guide buttons at the top right of this window explain the hub itself.

The window remembers which destination was open. A destination that cannot be built says why and offers Retry.

## Settings that are saved at once or on Save

The panels keep their own rules: Acquisition stores every change at once, ChiSurf Settings and Plots wait for Save or Apply, Updates and Packages run commands only when you press their buttons.
