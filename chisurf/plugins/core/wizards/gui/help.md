# Wizards

A launcher for ChiSurf's guided workflows. The list on the left names the wizards; the one you pick is drawn on the right under its description.

## Using the hub

- Click an entry (or press the up and down arrow keys) to open a wizard. It is built the first time and keeps its state, so you can switch to the other one and back without losing your place.
- Files dropped on the window go to the open wizard.
- Each wizard has its own **Help** and **Guide** buttons.
- The selected wizard and the settings of the wizards you opened are remembered.

## The wizards

- **Anisotropy** - build a linked VV/VH global time-resolved anisotropy fit. See [Lifetime and anisotropy fitting](docs/guides/10_lifetime_anisotropy_fitting.md).
- **Batch analysis** - apply one template fit to many datasets or files. See [Model comparison and batch fits](docs/guides/78_model_comparison_and_batch.md).
