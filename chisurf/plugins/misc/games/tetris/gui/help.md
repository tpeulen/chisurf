# Tetris

Place falling pieces to complete rows. The spectral colours and score are
part of the game; the board does not analyse fluorescence data or estimate a
sample property. Pieces and the board are generated by the game itself.

## Controls

- Hold Left/Right to move a piece horizontally.
- Hold Down for a faster descent.
- Press Up to rotate, or Space to drop the piece immediately.
- P pauses or resumes. R and the header Restart button start a fresh round.
- Sound can enable a supplied audio output; it is unavailable when none exists.

## Reading the round

Completed rows contribute to the score and level. A piece that can no longer
be placed ends the round. The board, current piece and status remain in the
canvas below the header. Guide points at the real Restart and Help controls
and waits for your clicks; opening a guide does not move or reset a piece.

## State and sound

The native host can retain the board, active piece, score and window size.
The Games window keeps this round alive when another game is selected. Losing
focus clears held keys and repeat timing. No audio device is opened by the
native game itself. If an optional audio output is supplied through Python,
focus loss silences it while preserving the saved Sound preference, and closing
releases it once.
