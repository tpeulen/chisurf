# Calculators

The hub groups ChiSurf's standalone calculators in one window. Click a calculator in the list on the left to open it on the right, under its description. Each calculator is built the first time it is opened and keeps its inputs while another one is shown.

## The list

Hover an entry to read what the calculator does. Up and Down move the selection when no field of the calculator is being edited. The calculator that cannot be built shows the reason under the description instead of a blank page; pick another entry to continue.

## Using a calculator

Every calculator has its own Guide and Help buttons, its own fields and its own plots; the hub only hosts it. A file dropped on the window goes to the calculator on the right. The hub remembers which calculator was open and the inputs of the calculators you opened.
