# LLTF parameter recovery (campaign slice 1, 2026-10-24)

Owner directive 2026-10-24: LLTF "was never battle tested; now it can be tested
with tttrlib improved sim capability". This record is the first campaign slice:
parameter recovery on decays generated by LLTF's own forward model
(`convolve_lifetime_spectrum`, the convolution the fitter inverts), Gaussian IRF
(σ = 4 channels, centred at channel 60), 1024 channels × 32 ps, peak 2 × 10⁴
counts, Poisson noise, three noise seeds per case, seeded starts (seed 0).

## Measured recovery (arm64 env, 2026-10-24)

| case | spectrum (amp, τ / ns) | worst rel-τ error | worst abs-amp error | χ²ᵣ range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mono | (1.0, 4.0) | 0.0008 | < 10⁻⁴ | 0.91 – 0.99 |
| bi | (0.6, 0.5), (0.4, 4.0) | 0.0031 | 0.0016 | 0.89 – 0.99 |
| bi-close | (0.5, 1.0), (0.5, 2.0) | 0.0553 | 0.0439 | 0.69 – 1.53 |
| tri | (0.5, 0.4), (0.3, 2.0), (0.2, 8.0) | 0.0268 | 0.0044 | 0.92 – 1.04 |

Well-separated components are recovered to a few 10⁻³ relative; components a
factor two apart (τ = 1.0 vs 2.0 ns) degrade to ≈ 5 % worst case with one seed
at χ²ᵣ 1.5 — the expected ill-conditioning of a two-exponential with close
lifetimes, not a defect of the optimiser.

## Guard

`test/core/test_lltf_recovery.py` pins the matrix above with tolerances that
carry ≈ 3× head-room on the measured worst case (mono/bi 2 %, tri 5 %,
bi-close 10 %; amplitudes 0.01, bi-close 0.06; χ²ᵣ < 2).

## What this slice does not cover (campaign continues)

Photon-level truth from tttrlib `Sim*` (routing, micro-time quantisation,
afterpulsing-style artefacts) and the fret_check SCENARIOS measurements;
anisotropy-coupled (VV/VH) recovery; IRF-mismatch bias (fitting with a wrong
IRF width); low-count regimes (peak < 10³). The campaign entry in
[known issues](known-issues.md) stays open until those land.
