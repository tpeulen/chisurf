# Imaging Tools

Begin in Browser to select a photon image. Define detector channels and PIE windows
in Setup once; every imaging step receives that definition. Intensity creates the
shared imaging HDF5. Number & Brightness, Mean Micro-Time and Phasor enrich that
same measurement. IRF & BG supplies optional per-detector calibration.

Navigate freely or use Previous and Next. Each opened tool retains its settings,
drawn regions and results. A pending route remains visible until its native child
is available. Jobs, progress, cancellation and errors are shown within each child.

Use Search to filter the navigation. Hover over actions and routes for descriptions.
