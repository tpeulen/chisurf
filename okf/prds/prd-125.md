# One Gaussian-mixture EM

# Where to pick this up (2026-09-03, after review)

`d7ae24ea5` landed a real shared kernel (`core/ml/_gaussian.py`:
`_row_logsumexp`, `_responsibilities`) and forwarded **two** of the three
call sites (`ml/mixture`, `fret/accurate._em_1d` — the hand-rolled loop is
gone). Open, in order:

1. **The HMM M-step forked again instead of forwarding** — `math/hmm.py`
   grew its own `ConvergenceMonitor._gain` and its own zero-row handling,
   with a `-inf` guard *different* from the kernel's (`_gain` returns 0.0
   on equality; `_row_logsumexp` returns `-inf`). This is the fourth copy
   the job warned about. Route it through `_gaussian` or record why not.
2. The two HMM trap cases are not pinned against the shared kernel (only
   the `-inf − -inf` case is); nothing tests the new `_gain` or the
   keep-previous empty-row behaviour.
3. Placement: the kernel landed in-tree, but the job said engine-side
   (tttrlib math). Either move it or record the decision here.
4. No timing.

Note the tree was red for 4 commits because the kernel's test file landed
on `b729833af` (PRD-124) before the kernel existed — the
commit-discipline lesson for the next slice.


# Why

Phase-4 register row: three EM implementations
(`ml/mixture/_gaussian_mixture.py`, `fret/accurate.py::_em_1d`, the
M-step inside `math/hmm.py`). The HMM's in-tree seam carries known traps
(`-inf − -inf = nan`, dead-state 0/0, frozen Dirichlet zeros) that the
other two copies do not share — which is exactly how three copies of one
algorithm disagree silently.

# The job

1. Parity A/B of the three at current behaviour on shared fixtures
   (well-separated mixture; degenerate empty-component case; the HMM trap
   cases above).
2. One kernel, engine side (tttrlib math is the placement-rule owner for
   this — it is data reduction over photon-derived quantities); the
   chisurf callers keep their parameter surfaces.
3. Coordinate the HMM M-step with [PRD-77](prd-77.md)/tttrlib PRD-013
   (the whole EM iteration engine-side) — this PRD must not create a
   fourth copy that PRD-77 then replaces; if PRD-77 lands first, this row
   shrinks to the two non-HMM callers.
4. `core/ml/` direction (PRD-105 decision 4) is **not** claimed here — 
   only the mixture EM row.
