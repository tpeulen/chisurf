# One Gaussian-mixture EM

# Why

Phase-4 register row: three EM implementations
(`ml/mixture/_gaussian_mixture.py`, `fret/accurate.py::_em_1d`, the
M-step inside `math/hmm.py`). The HMM's in-tree seam carries known traps
(`-inf − -inf = nan`, dead-state 0/0, frozen Dirichlet zeros) that the
other two copies do not share — which is exactly how three copies of one
algorithm disagree silently.

# The job

1. Parity A/B of the three at current behaviour on shared fixtures
   (well-separated mixture; degenerate empty-component case; the HMM trap
   cases above).
2. One kernel, engine side (tttrlib math is the placement-rule owner for
   this — it is data reduction over photon-derived quantities); the
   chisurf callers keep their parameter surfaces.
3. Coordinate the HMM M-step with [PRD-77](prd-77.md)/tttrlib PRD-013
   (the whole EM iteration engine-side) — this PRD must not create a
   fourth copy that PRD-77 then replaces; if PRD-77 lands first, this row
   shrinks to the two non-HMM callers.
4. `core/ml/` direction (PRD-105 decision 4) is **not** claimed here — 
   only the mixture EM row.

# Definition of done

- [ ] One EM implementation reachable from all three call sites; the
      other bodies deleted.
- [ ] The three trap cases pinned in tests against the one kernel.
- [ ] Before/after timing; ground-truth recovery unchanged.
- [ ] Register row struck in [PRD-105](prd-105.md).
