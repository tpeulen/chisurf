# The bounds transform must not stall on decade-spanning bounds

# Why

Found 2026-09-02 writing the ICS recovery test (recorded in
[known-issues](../references/known-issues.md)): with `bounds_on=True` and
the parameter groups' defensive defaults (`ub=1e9`, widths to `1e5`), a
2D-Gaussian fit from a *close* start stops at chi2r ≈ 600 where the
unbounded problem converges to chi2r ≈ 1.0 in six evaluations. Graph and
director stall **identically** — it is the shared sin/sqrt-style
reparameterisation, whose Jacobian columns for a huge interval sit
kilometres from the others' scale. Any bounded fit with wide bounds can
silently under-converge; the fit *finishes*, which is what makes it
dangerous.

# The job

1. Reproduce minimally in `imp.bff` (the ICS fixture from
   `test/fitting/test_graph_fit_ics.py` transplants directly), then
   instrument: is it transform curvature, MINPACK's relative step under
   the transform, or diagonal scaling?
2. Fix in the engine's bound handling — candidates, in order of
   preference: (a) projected/clipped steps instead of a smooth transform
   (what scipy's `trf` does); (b) per-parameter step scaling aware of the
   transform's local derivative; (c) internal rescaling of the transform
   to the start value's decade. One implementation, both paths inherit it.
3. Re-run the ICS 2D-Gaussian recovery **with bounds on** and the DEER /
   TCSPC / FCS graph suites (answers must not move on well-posed fits).

# Definition of done

- [x] The ICS 2D-Gaussian fixture converges to chi2r ≈ 1 with the group's

- [x] `test/fitting/test_graph_fit_ics.py` drops its bounds-off

# The fix

Landed candidate (b): per-parameter step scaling aware of the transform's
local derivative — `IMP.bff.Minimizer::fdjac2_step` (new,
`modules/bff/src/Minimizer.cpp` + `include/Minimizer.h` in the `imp` fork
that carries the `bff` module; **not** a chisurf-tree file, see "Where this
actually lives" below).

`fdjac2`'s forward-difference step was `eps * |xi|` in the *internal*
(transformed) coordinate for every bounded parameter alike. For a two-sided
bound the transform is `xe = lo + (ub-lo)/2 * (sin(xi)+1)`, whose derivative
is `(ub-lo)/2 * cos(xi)` — on the order of the *half-width* almost
everywhere over a decade-spanning interval, collapsing to zero only right at
the two edges. A fixed relative step in `xi` therefore probes the *external*
parameter by an amount set by the box width, not by the parameter's own
scale: kilometres away for the ICS `A0` case (bound `(0, 1e9)`, value `0.4`).

First attempt: always take the step that is `eps * |xe|` in *external*
units, inverted through `to_internal`. This fixed the ICS fixture (chi2r
608 → 1.03, matching unbounded) but **regressed** a TCSPC lifetime fit whose
scatter fraction converges near its `(0, 100)` lower bound — chi2r 3.10
(scipy `trf` independently agrees: 3.09) moved to 3.25, caught by
`test/fitting/test_graph_fit_tcspc.py::test_a_round_off_column_does_not_become_an_error_bar`'s
own precondition failing. Right at a genuine edge the *original* internal
step is already the safer one (the transform's derivative is shrinking to
zero there too), and the externally-relative step overshoots instead by
falling back to a fixed absolute `eps` once `|xe|` is tiny.

Final: take whichever of the two candidate steps is **smaller in
magnitude** — the more conservative, more locally-linear probe either way.
Deep in an oversized box (ICS) the internal step overshoots and the
external one wins; right at an edge (TCSPC) the external step overshoots
and the internal one — already correctly shrunk by the transform — wins.
Scoped to the two-sided branch only; one-sided and unbounded parameters are
bit-identical to before.

## Where this actually lives

The bound transform is **not** in `chisurf/`. `chisurf/core/fitting/minimizer.py`
only calls into `IMP.bff.Minimizer` (`m.bounds = ...`, `m.run()`); the
transform, `fdjac2`, and the whole bounded-LM loop are C++ in the `imp` fork
that vendors the `bff` module (`modules/bff/src/Minimizer.cpp` /
`include/Minimizer.h`), a *separate git repository* from both chisurf and
`imp-tricks`. No chisurf-tree file needed to change for this PRD — verified
by grepping `minimizer.py` for the transform (`sin`/`asin`/`to_external`
etc.): none of it is there.

# Traps

- The bounded reference behaviour is pinned by
  `test_bounds_are_honoured` — bounds must still bind exactly, only the
  path to the interior minimum may change.
- `reference_leastsqbound.py` (imp.bff test/) is the frozen historical
  transform: parity against it is *not* a goal here — this PRD exists
  because that transform's behaviour is the defect.
- **A step formula that is only "more correct" in the abstract can still
  regress a real fit.** The externally-relative step is the right idea deep
  in an oversized box and the wrong one right at a genuine edge; the fix
  needs both regimes, not the more elegant-looking one alone. Caught only
  by running the *other* bounded-fit suites (TCSPC), not the ICS fixture
  this PRD started from — a single fixture is not enough evidence for an
  engine-wide change.
