# Why

Phase-6 governance row. The graph-objective seam
(`chisurf/core/fitting/minimizer.py`) now carries: the parse builder with
model-declared axes, the lifetime/FRET builder with its producer chain,
pile-up and DNL stages, group objectives, mask representation, the graph
cache and its key, curve publication, curvature-over-the-graph, and the
refusal contract. None of that has a subsystem concept; the knowledge
lives in docstrings, imp.bff's log, and PRD-105 — which is a *plan*, and
plans get superseded.

# The job

1. `okf/subsystems/graph-objective.md` (new): what builds, what refuses
   and why each refusal is semantic, the port/keepalive lifetime rules
   (`%pythonappend`, `_graph`, `_sampler_surface`), the cache key's
   contents and its deliberate omissions, the publication path, and the
   protocols a model can implement (`_expression`/`_parameters_equation`,
   `graph_axes()`, `func` regeneration). Point every "see PRD-105" in
   nearby code comments here instead (the no-PRDs-in-source rule).
2. Rewrite `okf/subsystems/fitting.md` to the post-migration shape: Fit
   lifecycle, the sampler's four-crossing contract, `objective_type`,
   support-plane thresholds, and where the graph seam hands off.
3. Cross-link both from `compute-display-line.md` and the family
   verdicts; register in `okf/index.md`.
