# MFD spectrum algebra onto the graph's own producers

# Verification (2026-09-03, review pass)

Landed by `f0dc3ed59`; audit clean on the substance: donor spectra drive
`IMP.bff.FretSpectrum`, polarisation drives `AnisotropySpectrum` with the
`1 + 2r0` trap handled by citation not rediscovery, VV/VH parity pinned
with rotations armed and a G-factor-independence matrix. Two deliberate
deviations were stated loudly in docstrings but pointed at known-issues
entries that did not exist — **the review pass wrote them**
(`AnisotropySpectrum` G-before-mixing ordering; the missing
`AcceptorSpectrum` node that keeps `acceptor_lifetime_spectrum` a numpy
transform). The first DoD box is checked with that one recorded exception.


# Why

Phase-4 register row. `mfd/patterns.py` builds lifetime spectra, Perrin
anisotropy and polarised decay patterns in numpy — the same photophysics
`IMP.bff` `FretSpectrum`/`AnisotropySpectrum` compute as the TCSPC
graph's producers (the product of two sums of exponentials is a sum of
exponentials; a VV/VH pattern is a spectrum transform). Two owners of the
same photophysics is the `MaxEntLifetimeModel` failure shape.

# The job

1. Map each pattern function onto the producer-node algebra; anything
   genuinely missing (e.g. a pattern-specific normalisation) goes into
   the bff node, not beside it.
2. Parity A/B per pattern — VV *and* VH, with rotation terms armed;
   magic-angle-only fixtures prove half of a polarised setup (the census
   lesson).
3. Forward, delete the numpy bodies; the MFD callers keep their surfaces.
