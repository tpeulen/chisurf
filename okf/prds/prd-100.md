# PRD-100 — The accessible-volume stack is re-expressed against IMP, not deleted

**Status:** ✏️ draft — written 2026-08-11 so the work is scoped before anyone
starts it.
**Blocks:** four of the thirteen files on
[numba retirement](../subsystems/numba-retirement.md)'s allow-list —
`core/structure/av/static.py`, `av/dynamic.py`, `structure/potential/potentials.py`,
`structure/protein.py` — plus `av/functions.py`, whose route is `wgsl` but whose
9 kernels are entangled with the same data structures.
**Related:** [PRD-99](prd-99.md) asks whether the AV *approximation* holds
against an IMP Boltzmann ensemble. This PRD is the other half of the same
subsystem — whether the AV *implementation* should be ChiSurf's or IMP's — and
the two meet at `IMP.bff.AV`. PRD-99's precondition (the `set_av_parameter`
radius bug) applies here too: fitting or comparing volumes through it would
absorb the error somewhere harmless-looking.

**Do not start before:** PRD-97 settles. A peer has ~159 uncommitted lines in
`structure/protein.py` handing `fret/core`'s algorithms to `IMP.bff.fret`, and
that work may decide where some of this belongs.

## Why this exists

The allow-list said these files were "already migrated to imp-tricks under the
same function names — delete the leftover". **That is false, and it was checked
rather than argued** (2026-08-11): none of the symbols exist in the installed
`IMP.bff`, `IMP.cgmol` or `IMP.bff.cgdye`, nor in `~/dev/imp.bff`, exactly or
fuzzily.

| ChiSurf | in IMP? |
|---|---|
| `centroid2`, `internal_potential`, `lj_calpha`, `gb`, `go` | no |
| `make_grid_axis`, `find_atom_clashes`, `define_starting_positions`, `distance_lookup`, `calc_linker_distance` | no |
| `_quenching_rate_per_frame` | no |
| `internal_to_cartesian` | no |

What *does* exist is `IMP.bff.AV` — a real accessible-volume decorator
(`get_linker_length`, `get_linker_width`, `get_map`, `get_mean_position`,
`create_path_map_header`, `get_allowed_sphere_radius`) — and
`IMP.cgmol.ProteinCentroid` / `ProteinMC`. So the **direction** is right and the
**work** is not what the label says: ChiSurf's kernels are the *internals* of an
AV calculation, so this is re-expressing ChiSurf against a different API and
proving the physics did not move — not deleting a duplicate.

Getting this wrong is expensive in a specific way: an AV that is subtly
different still produces a plausible ⟨R_DA⟩ and a plausible FRET efficiency.
Nothing raises.

## What is in scope

Three groups, which should land separately:

1. **The AV grid** — `av/static.py` (`make_grid_axis`, `find_atom_clashes`,
   `define_starting_positions`, `distance_lookup`, `calc_linker_distance`) and
   the `av/functions.py` kernels that consume its density
   (`density2points`, `split_av_acv`, `random_distances`). This is what
   `IMP.bff.AV` replaces if anything does.
2. **The dye-diffusion / quenching maps** — `av/dynamic.py`'s
   `_quenching_rate_per_frame` and `av/functions.py`'s
   `assign_diffusion_to_grid_*`, `iterate_cpu`, `reduce_decay_cpu`,
   `create_fret_rate_map`, `create_quenching_map`. These model dye photophysics
   on the AV grid; whether IMP wants them at all is an open question this PRD
   does not answer.
3. **The ProteinMC potentials** — `potentials.py`'s `centroid2`,
   `internal_potential`, `lj_calpha`, `gb`, `go`, and `protein.py`'s
   `internal_to_cartesian`. **This group has no IMP target at all today**:
   `IMP.bff` exposes only `AVNetworkRestraint`. `GoPotential`, `HPotential` and
   `Ramachandran` are live behind the ProteinMC model and three GUI widgets
   (`gui/widgets/structure/potentials_{go,hbond,rama}.py`), so they cannot be
   deleted and have nowhere to go. Either IMP grows them or they stay and take
   a different numba route.

## The consumers that must not move

An AV change is felt here, and each is a place to assert parity rather than
hope:

- `core/models/tcspc/av_decay.py` and `fret_structure.py` — AV-derived decay
  models.
- `plugins/modelling/fret/` — `core/trajectory.py`, `evaluators/base.py`, and
  the FPS-style outputs (`test_fps_outputs.py`).
- `plugins/modelling/fps_json_editor/` — the position panel and its AV worker.
- `gui/widgets/fluorescence/av/` and `gui/widgets/structure/potentials_av.py`.

## The parity bar

Not "the tests still pass" — the tests were written against the current
implementation. Each group carries its own comparison, recorded **before** the
kernels are deleted, the same way the maximum-entropy and 2D-FDC ports did:

1. **The AV grid itself.** For a fixed structure, label and linker geometry:
   identical occupied-voxel count, identical mean position to 1e-9 Å, and an
   identical density array. The grid is deterministic, so this is exact — if it
   cannot be made exact, the difference must be explained before it is accepted.
2. **⟨R_DA⟩ and ⟨R_DA⟩_E for a real pair**, on T4 Lysozyme (PDB 148L), against
   the recorded values. These are what users publish; a shift here is the
   failure this PRD exists to prevent.
3. **The dye-diffusion decay** — the quenched donor decay from
   `iterate_cpu`/`reduce_decay_cpu` on a fixed grid, compared curve-for-curve.
4. **A simulation with a known answer**, not only recorded numbers: two
   labelling sites at a known separation in a structure with no quenchers must
   return that separation within the grid spacing. A recorded-value comparison
   cannot tell a faithful port from a shared mistake — the lesson PRD-035 paid
   for when a fixture recorded from the code under replacement encoded a live
   bug.

## What this PRD deliberately does not decide

- **Whether group 2 belongs in IMP at all.** Dye photophysics on a grid may be
  ChiSurf's own subject matter. Decide it with the AV grid in hand, not before.
- **Where group 3 goes** if IMP does not want the potentials. `tttr-c` is
  wrong (they are not photon kernels); plain NumPy may be right if they are not
  hot — which is a measurement nobody has taken.
- **The `wgsl` route for `av/functions.py`.** The AV grid is a large uniform 3-D
  grid, one-shot, independent per voxel, which is exactly the WGSL case; but it
  is moot if `IMP.bff.AV` computes the grid instead. Settle group 1 first.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Group 1: the AV grid comes from `IMP.bff.AV`, with the recorded-grid and
      ⟨R_DA⟩ comparisons green and the known-separation simulation passing.
- [ ] Group 2: decided (migrate, keep, or WGSL) with the measurement that
      decided it written down.
- [ ] Group 3: decided, with the potentials either in IMP or on a stated route.
- [ ] The four files leave `test/numba_import_allowlist.txt`, and
      `av/functions.py` leaves or has its `wgsl` route confirmed.
- [ ] [numba retirement](../subsystems/numba-retirement.md) records the outcome
      of each group, including any that stayed.
